A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Visit the Help Center
Is Editor really free?+

Yes. Editor is free and open source under the MIT license. All current workspaces are included, with no subscription, paid tier, or export watermark.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?+

No account is required. Projects and imported media are stored locally, and the core editing workflow runs on your computer. Downloads, online media sources, and the first download of a caption transcription model need an internet connection.

What can I do with captions?+

Generate captions with the local transcription engine, or import SRT, VTT, and ASS subtitles. Edit words and timings, split or merge cues, and style the text. Export plain SRT/VTT sidecars or render your styled captions into the video.

Can I move my project into another editor?+

You can create handoff packages for Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, and experimentally CapCut. Editable transfers preserve supported timeline properties; advanced effects and grades are not recreated as native controls. Choose Preserve appearance to render the finished look into the transfer. Read the transfer guide.

Is there a mobile or browser version?+

This release is a desktop app for macOS, Windows, and Linux. The website is a tour of the product. It doesn’t run the editor or upload your footage.

How can I help make Editor better?+

Share a reproducible bug, suggest a workflow improvement, or contribute code. Editor is an independently maintained fork of OpenCut and is still evolving. Learn about the project.
