## Production prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand. Edit target: existing Editor desktop
app logo in the supplied image. Create the production-ready replacement app icon,
one single icon only. Preserve the exact recognizable identity and orientation of
the original: a tall vertical right-hand stroke bending left into a sideways
heart-shaped looping ribbon, with the second lower right stem and the same
crossing. Keep its essential silhouette, proportions and heart/loop negative
space; do not replace it with scissors, letters or an unrelated icon. Make the
strokes roughly 25% thicker than the original, consistently weighted and optically
balanced. Make the mark larger inside its container for excellent legibility at
24, 32 and 64 pixels. Style: extremely clean, precise vector-like geometry, smooth
antialiased edges, flat solid colors. Replace white glyph with uniform saturated
yellow #FFD600. Replace the textured rounded-square container with a true square
near-black #0B0B0B tile, perfectly straight sides and sharp 90-degree corners. The
black tile fills the full square image edge-to-edge. Glyph centered optically,
spans approximately 76% of canvas height with generous even breathing room, shape
width stays proportional to its height. Use subtly squared off ends rather than
pill-shaped caps, but preserve the graceful loop. Show the ribbon crossing with a
tiny deliberate black separation gap rather than gray shading. No gradients,
grain, texture, highlights, drop shadows, bevels, 3D, lettering, border, mockup,
perspective, watermarks or presentation sheet. Output one polished 2048x2048
square app icon.
